PvE Ranked league identity polish

- Kreisliga / Bezirksliga / Regionalliga now have separate funny-name pools.
- Bundesliga mixes the remaining funny names with recognizable football players.
- Champions League uses top football-star names such as Haaland, Mbappe, Messi,
  Ronaldo, Salah, Vinicius, Bellingham, De Bruyne, Neymar and Rodri.
- Theodore League uses all-time / ultra-elite football identities around its
  guaranteed single boss.
- Upper-division visitors are now capped at exactly one and use a controlled
  one-in-five-match cadence instead of the old near-promotion 45% roll that
  could occasionally spawn two.
- The visitor gets the next division's intelligence, MMR and identity pool.
- Champions boss encounters do not stack a Theodore visitor on top.
- Existing Intelligence 1-20 league ranges and boss rules are otherwise kept.
