PvE Ranked Footballer Playstyle Profiles

Purpose
- Real footballer names now carry gameplay identity instead of only a display name.
- Reuses the existing CPU personality, ability-role and defensive-role systems.

What changes
- Footballer names receive compatible ability pools based on their real-world archetype.
- Signature players have tighter kits (Messi, Haaland, Mbappe, Ronaldo, KDB, Neymar/Ronaldinho, Rodri, Roberto Carlos, Beckham).
- Footballers carry a field-role tag: goalkeeper, defender, holding, midfielder, creator, winger, striker or forward_creator.
- Goalkeeper/final-defender selection respects that role.
- Emergency defensive rotations are still allowed when positioning requires them.
- Champions League adds Van Dijk, Rudiger, Courtois and Alisson so elite teams can form proper defensive structures instead of forcing forwards into defensive kits.
- Theodore League also includes Neuer and Buffon as goalkeeper legends.

Important
- Boss profiles remain untouched.
- Existing Intelligence 1-20 tuning remains untouched.
- Existing PvE Ranked league-name pools and upper-division guest rules remain intact apart from the added defensive star names above.
