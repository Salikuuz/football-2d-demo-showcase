HYBRID AI CHECKPOINT RESUME FIX V2

WHY V1 FAILED

V1 checked the checksum of the raw policy JSON. Older trainer saves could
calculate their checksum from a normalized in-memory policy but write a
different/sparser policy document. Therefore neither the raw policy nor the
policy normalized with today's newer defaults necessarily matches the old
checksum.

V2 safely accepts that specific legacy format only when:
- checkpoint, observation, and action-space versions match
- model_type is hybrid_linear_tactical_policy
- training counters are non-negative
- bias and weight dictionaries are structurally valid
- enough known tactical actions are present

It then immediately normalizes the policy, creates a timestamped byte-for-byte
backup, rewrites active.json atomically, and verifies the new checksum.
