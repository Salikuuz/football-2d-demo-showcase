SIDE SWIPE - PLAYMAKER ABILITY (ID 21)

Controls:
1. Press Ability to arm Side Swipe for 4 seconds.
2. Hold the movement direction toward the side you want.
3. Press Pass for a sharp lateral pass.
4. Or charge/release Shoot for a harder lateral shot.
5. If no clear side input is held, the ability selects the safer lateral lane.

Also included:
- Side Swipe in team ability selection.
- Side Swipe in Freeplay ability selection.
- HUD, scoreboard, preview and CPU support.
- Updated Breakaway icon: running/dribbling player instead of an arrow.
